# Project Brief: Tuma Mobile App (MVP)

## Project Overview
**Tuma** (meaning "Send" in Swahili) is a delivery and errand-running platform designed to connect users with reliable "Runners" for quick task fulfillment. The app emphasizes speed, reliability, and professional service through a clean, high-contrast visual identity.

---

## Brand Identity & Design System

### Visual Direction
The brand identity is centered around the concept of movement and delivery. 
- **Logo**: A creative, geometric "T" that incorporates a stylized paper plane/runner icon, symbolizing forward motion.
- **Color Palette**: 
  - **Mustard Yellow (#E8A820)**: Primary brand color used for key actions, buttons, and loading indicators.
  - **Ink Black (#1A1A1A)**: Used for high-contrast text and primary brand elements.
  - **Surface White (#FFFFFF)**: The primary background color for a clean, professional aesthetic.
- **Typography**: **Hanken Grotesk**. A modern, geometric sans-serif that balances readability with brand personality.
- **Corner Radius**: **16px (rounded-2xl)**. Applied to buttons, input fields, and cards to create a modern, approachable feel.

---

## User Flows: Authentication & Onboarding

### 1. Initial Entry
- **Splash Screen**: Features the centered Tuma brand mark with a Mustard Yellow loading indicator against a solid white background.
- **Onboarding Carousel**: A multi-step introduction explaining the "Send" concept and how to get started.

### 2. Registration & Role Selection
- **Role Selection**: Users choose between being a "Customer" or a "Runner." This screen includes a mandatory Terms and Conditions checkbox.
- **Sign Up**: A streamlined form capturing Name, Phone Number, and Email, with options for Google SSO.

### 3. Verification & Security
- **OTP Verification**: A centered numeric input field for 6-digit codes with a "Resend Now" option positioned directly below for accessibility.
- **Runner ID Verification**: Specifically for the Runner role, capturing ID numbers and document uploads. The layout is optimized to keep primary fields at the top of the screen.
- **Log In & Forgot Password**: Standard secure entry points with consistent branding and high-contrast Mustard Yellow calls-to-action.
